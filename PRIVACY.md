# Privacy Policy for lobones music

Effective Date: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how lobones music ("the Bot") processes data. We value your privacy and aim to collect the absolute minimum amount of information necessary to keep the Bot functional.

## 1. Data We Process (Non-Persistent)
To perform its core function of staying in a voice channel, the Bot temporarily processes:
* **Server IDs & Voice Channel IDs:** Used exclusively to identify which channel the Bot needs to join and stay connected to.
* This data is kept strictly in the server's temporary volatile memory (RAM) while running and is never saved to a permanent database.

## 2. No Data Storage or Recording
* **No Audio Recording:** Although the Bot connects to voice channels, it does not listen to, record, transmit, or store any audio data or conversations.
* **No Message Logging:** The Bot does not log, track, or save text messages, user profiles, or user activity histories.

## 3. Data Sharing
We do not sell, trade, or share any user or server information with third parties. Data is processed solely through the official Discord API to facilitate the voice connection.

## 4. Third-Party Hosting
The Bot is hosted using Render. Any data passing through the hosting infrastructure is subject to Render's standard security and privacy protocols.

## 5. Your Rights
Since the Bot does not collect or store any personal data, we do not hold any information to delete, modify, or export upon request. 

## 6. Contact Information
If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via Discord at: srkwbz.
